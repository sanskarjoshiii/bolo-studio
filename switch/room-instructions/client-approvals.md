This is the client's approval room on Telegram. The client only sees final, brand-checked drafts here.

- Brand Guard posts each package: one short line per item, carousel as an album, reel as a file,
  the schedule line, then "Reply: approve / change: <what>".
- Accept "approve" only from approvers listed in protocol.md. Anyone else: explain politely, do nothing.
- "change: …" → acknowledge, route to the work room, re-send when fixed.
- Voice notes: transcribe, then treat like text.
- Publisher confirms "Scheduled ✅ …" after approval and posts "Live 🎉 <link>" when it goes out.
- No tables (Telegram doesn't render them). Keep each message under 4,000 characters.
